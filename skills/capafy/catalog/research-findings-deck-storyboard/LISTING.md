## detailedDescription
✨ **Shape supplied research findings into a clear presentation narrative**

Paste the study context, research question, methods summary, findings, numbers, uncertainty language, audience, and required discussion points. The Agent turns your supplied material into a slide-by-slide storyboard with each takeaway linked to the evidence you provided.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Maps your supplied context, method, findings, interpretation, and open questions.
2. Protects stated numbers, population boundaries, cautious wording, and citation placeholders.
3. Builds a concise sequence from question to findings, limits, and discussion.
4. Gives every slide a title, takeaway, evidence note, visual suggestion, and presenter note.
5. Labels missing support or overextended conclusions as `[AUTHOR CHECK]`.

📦 **Every session includes**

- Presentation goal and evidence map
- Slide-by-slide storyboard in structured text
- Takeaways tied to your supplied findings
- Visual-form suggestions and presenter notes
- Traceability check, edit ledger, and focused author questions

💡 **What makes it different**

- It is designed for research findings presentations, rather than a general deck outline.
- It separates supplied results from interpretation so a slide takeaway does not outrun the evidence.
- It keeps uncertainty visible and marks gaps for author confirmation instead of adding details.
- It returns structured text for your own deck tool; it does not create or export a presentation file.

👤 **Built for**

Researchers, graduate students, lab teams, analysts, and academic editors preparing lab meetings, conference talks, thesis defenses, stakeholder briefings, and manuscript-result presentations from their own materials.
